# Crow Media Player

An iPhone-style media player card for Home Assistant, with the integration that runs it: deep Music Assistant support, synced lyrics, AI track and film information, podcasts, radio and audiobooks, Listening Recaps, PDF exports, a screensaver, a visualiser and an Apple TV remote.

**One download installs both.** The card is bundled inside the integration and loaded into your dashboards automatically.

## What the integration adds

- Everyone's pins, starred stations, saved queues, lyrics and AI answers kept in Home Assistant, following each person between devices
- Listening Recap recorded by Home Assistant, so music played from anywhere counts, even with every screen off
- A sleep timer that runs in Home Assistant and keeps going with the dashboard closed
- Wake-up alarms that start quietly and get louder over a minute
- Outside services fetched and cached by Home Assistant, with your TheMovieDB key and Discogs token kept there
- AI requests through Home Assistant, with reusable answers shared by the house
- Made for You mixes made each week from what the house listens to: On Repeat, Your Month in Music, Forgotten Favourites, Discover Weekly and mood mixes
- Resume: carry on where a speaker stopped, after a pause, switching off or a power cut
- Full screen for a tablet, phone or wall screen, with scrolling synced lyrics, the next song, the Apple TV remote as a third panel, the app's icon and a summary for apps such as Netflix, and an exit button that always works
- Music Assistant's own queue with Queue Actions: the whole queue, Radio Mode as Music Assistant's Don't stop the music, pinned queues saved as Music Assistant playlists
- Notifications for new episodes of pinned podcasts and new releases by the artists you play most
- Sensors: Music playing, Listening time, Tracks played today, Top artist and track this week, Last played, Listening streak, New podcast episodes, New releases, Rooms playing, Last music, Listening by room, and for each speaker Sleep timer, Next alarm, Radio station, Queue time left, Now watching, Audiobook progress, Up next, Song ends at, Audio quality, Radio Mode and Something to resume
- Actions: start and cancel a sleep timer, set and delete alarms, play a station, play something pinned, play a mood, play a mix, make mixes, pause everywhere, resume, forget history, check for new episodes and releases

## The card

- An iPhone-style player in compact and full size, with skins and liquid glass
- Deep Music Assistant support: the library, queue, Radio Mode, saved queues, Artist Radio and fast enqueue
- Synced lyrics with translation
- AI Info for songs, albums, artists, films and TV, with Ask, Meaning, Story, Trivia, Mood Match and Spot It (AI features are optional and off by default)
- Podcasts, radio stations and LibriVox audiobooks, Music History, Watch History and Listening Recaps
- PDF exports, a screensaver, a visualiser, an Apple TV remote and keyboard

## Setup

1. Download, then restart Home Assistant
2. **Settings → Devices & Services → + Add Integration** → **Crow Media Player** → **Submit**
3. Optionally choose **Configure** for speakers, keys and notifications
4. Refresh your browser or app, then add the **Crow Media Player** card to a dashboard

Full documentation, and where to report problems: [github.com/jamesmcginnis/crow-media-player](https://github.com/jamesmcginnis/crow-media-player)
